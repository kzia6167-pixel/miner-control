Why Join Miner Control`s Ambassador Program

At Miner Control we give our users an opportunity to become our ambassadors. By becoming a Miner Control
Ambassador, you will be the representative and our eyes for the users and
help raise awareness about the many benefits of Miner Control.

Competitive

Get compensated generously for your hard work. Up to 10 % commision + fixed compensation

Exclusive

Hear firsthand about Miner Control’s features, official releases, and exclusive news.

Recognition

Become the official Miner Control representative in your home country.

Requirements

In order to apply for the Ambassador program, you must meet the following criteria. You must be active on Twitter and on different blockchain communities You should have a good knowledge of Bitcoin and the cryptocurrency space as well as a general understanding of the blockchain and related technologies (e.g. mining)

Bonus but not required: At least 1,000 Followers on Twitter At least 1,000 Subscribers on YouTube You own a blog site related to blockchain You have your own blockchain community

Your Responsibilities

As an Ambassador, you will promote Miner Control in your country and play a key role in building the local Miner Control community. Here’s what you’ll have to do: Nurture our local marketing and business relationships. Helping us attract followers to our social media pages Reach out to potential clients for our products. Actively promoting our service locally, for example supporting us in getting local media visibility Engage with influencers and the local community. Collaborate with the core MIner Control team to achieve all goals.

How to Become a Miner Control Ambassador

All you have to do is send us your resume and a cover letter explaining why you’d like to join our Ambassador Program.
